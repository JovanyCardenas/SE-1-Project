About Spartan Planner

Spartan Planner is a student-developed academic planning application designed to help San José State University students manage their coursework and stay organized.

Our Purpose

Our goal is to make academic planning easier by providing a centralized place to manage courses, assignments, exams, and important deadlines.

What Spartan Planner Offers

Students can organize their courses, track upcoming assignments, view their academic calendar, and upload syllabi to help manage their schedules.

Developed by Students, for Students

Spartan Planner is being developed as part of the CMPE 131 Software Engineering I course at San José State University.
